Discovery Questions

Use 3–4 per call. Frame with: What · How · Why · Tell me about… · Walk me through… · Help me understand…

Strategic Alignment
Is deeper strategic alignment with a partner like Adobe something relevant to your near-term priorities?
Tech Stack in Use
Walk me through your current environment — what platforms are you running today?
Problems with Current Stack
What are some of the challenges or limitations you're experiencing with your current tools?
Impact of Problems
How is that affecting your team's ability to hit their goals?
Ideal State
What would be the impact for you if you had a system that could solve the challenges you mentioned?
Active Projects & Timeline
If everything goes to plan and you find a solution you like, when would be the ideal go-live month?
Authority & Decision Making
Who else is typically involved when a decision like this gets made?
Budget
How are budget considerations handled internally for a project like this?
Competitive Landscape
What stage are you at in the evaluation process — are you looking at other solutions?
